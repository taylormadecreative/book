# PRODUCT.md — TAYLORMADE/BOOK

**register: brand** (index.html; portal.html and admin.html lean product)

## What it is
Direct-booking site and HoneyBook replacement for Nelson Taylor's photography and video work. Three surfaces: a cinematic marketing + booking page (the product IS the design), a tokenized client portal (status, invoices, Stripe payments, messages, delivery), and a studio dashboard for Nelson (pipeline, invoices, messages, files).

## Who it's for
Artists, labels, and brand operators in DFW and beyond who want cinema-grade visuals and are ready to book: music videos, brand content and commercials, photography, event coverage. They arrive from Instagram or a referral, on a phone, comparing Nelson to cheaper shooters. The page wins on proof (real films, real campaign stills), speed to book, and the feeling that this operation is a level above.

## Voice
First person, confident, partnership-not-vendor. "I direct, shoot, and edit." No corporate speak, no salesy urgency, no fake scarcity, no discounts. Soft closes.

## Offer structure
- Custom-quoted projects (music video, brand content, photography, events): inquiry wizard, quote and 50% deposit in the portal. NO public pricing for these.
- Digitals: flat $100, instant booking with immediate invoice (the one published price, authorized by Nelson).
- Standard terms allowed in copy: 50% deposit locks the date, two rounds of revisions, roughly two-week delivery.

## Hard rules
- Real work only: films and stills come from Nelson's public portfolio (TONI&GUY, Oribe, Neutrogena, Foot Locker, Takis, La Supreme, Marviano, Mariah.B, Secret Med Spa, Goldwell, Konjo, Diameco, MYX, KMS, R+Co, Blackgirl Sunscreen). Never invent clients, stats, or testimonials.
- Never name suppliers or tools as "how it's made."
- No street address or studio building name. "Dallas–Fort Worth / shooting everywhere" is the only location framing.
- Credentials that may be claimed: 14 years creative direction, CoSign Videographer of the Year 2020, Sony FX6 + A7RV rig.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages. Squarespace photographer templates. Fiverr/Upwork shooter pages. Anything that reads "AI made that." The studio site's Bold Pop look (this site is its dark cinematic sibling, not a clone).
