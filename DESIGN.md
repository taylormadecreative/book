# DESIGN.md — TAYLORMADE/BOOK

## Register and strategy
Brand register (index). Aesthetic: **"Viewfinder" cinematic HUD futurism**. A camera operator's world: near-black surface, warm white type, one gold. Committed color strategy: gold carries identity moments (CTAs, brackets, the digitals band) on a near-black field; never floods body copy. Film grain overlay, viewfinder corner brackets, ticking timecode, REC pulse, filmstrip sprockets.

## Color (OKLCH-minded; never #000/#fff)
- `--bg` #08080a, `--bg-2` #0e0e12, `--bg-3` #15151b (blue-black, warm-tinted)
- `--text` #f4f1ea warm white; `--muted` #a09c91; `--faint` #6d6a62
- `--gold` #e9b949 (deep #c2922a); `--green` #57d9a3 paid; `--red` #ff5c5c REC/errors only
- Gold on black >= 8:1 contrast. Solid gold for emphasis (no gradient text).

## Type
- Display: **Unbounded** 700/800, uppercase, tight leading. Hero clamps to ~7vw.
- Body/UI: **Familjen Grotesk** 400-700, body capped ~60ch.
- HUD microtype: **Martian Mono** 400/500, 0.55-0.65rem, 0.2em tracking, uppercase. Used for eyebrows, timecodes, captions, labels only.

## Motion (Emil-calibrated)
- transform/opacity only; ease `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)`; UI under 320ms; no bounce.
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography: pinned filmstrip horizontal scrub, hero parallax, staggered reveals, process line draw. ALL gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and killed on mobile where they fight touch scrolling.
- Custom cursor (gold dot + trailing ring) desktop-with-fine-pointer only. Magnetic pull on primary CTAs, max 6px translate.
- Micro: scale 0.97 on press, underline wipes, REC pulse, marquee pauses on hover.

## Layout
- Full-bleed sections, varied vertical rhythm (process tighter, work looser, digitals band loud).
- Filmstrip rail with sprocket borders for stills; lightbox on click with HUD caption.
- Asymmetric service rows (number / copy / tags), not card grids.
- Mobile-first: single column under 900px, filmstrip becomes swipe rail, scrub effects disabled.

## Banned (plus impeccable's bans)
Public pricing except the $100 digitals flat rate. Supplier names. Street addresses. Fake scarcity or invented stats. Purple gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric template, identical card grids, side-stripe borders, gradient text, em dashes in copy.

## Client portal (portal.html), redesigned 2026-10-21 from Nelson's approved mockup
Product register; his mockup (`Taylormade Creative headshot portal.png`, the delivered state) overrides the bans above: the eyebrow over the headline and the three equal photo cards are approved.
- **Layout:** ink top bar and hero (#0b0b0d) over a warm paper sheet (#f5f2eb, cards #fbf9f5) with 28px rounded top corners. The hero holds a stage headline, the project title with an outlined gold status pill, one gold action on the right, and a five-step tracker (gold check circles). Below: tabs (Photos & video / Project details / Messages / Invoices; gold underline) and a 382px right column ("Your project" facts with line icons, then the ink "Need a change?" card). One column below 1180px; the right column becomes two cards side by side, then stacks on phones.
- **Stages drive the hero:** picking proofs, photos ready (Download all, zipped in the browser), contract to sign, invoice to pay ("Lock in your date." for an upcoming deposit), picks sent, links delivered, booked (shoot day on the right), in the edit, new inquiry, closed. One gold action at most.
- **Type:** Anton (headlines, section and card titles), Familjen Grotesk (everything read or pressed), IBM Plex Mono (eyebrow and the CLIENT PORTAL tag), Unbounded 800 for the TAYLORMADE/CREATIVE wordmark only (loaded with `text=` so it's just those letters). Never Martian Mono.
- **Components:** final photos as cards with a near-square head-and-shoulders preview, an expand button and "name + Download" under it; sent picks fold into a "Your original selections" row once edits land; chat with Nelson's messages on paper and the client's own in ink; green "Paid" and gold "Due" chips.
- **Extra edits ($35/photo, `bk_config.extra_edit_cents`):** past the included picks, more taps become "Extra" (gold tag, local until confirmed, total in the pick bar and the confirm). After picks are sent, "Add extra edits" reopens the unpicked proofs as a chooser. One `bk_portal_request_extras` call marks them `extra`, creates an "Extra edits" invoice (source `portal_extras`, no "quote is ready" email) and posts the file list to Nelson. The hero then asks for the payment ("Lock in your extra edits.", or an outlined Pay under Download once photos are delivered). Phones swipe the finals sideways.
- **Storyboard review (2026-10-26, first used for Delilah Amaro's Hide & Haunt AI commercial):** a `storyboard` gallery reads as a shot list, not a photo grid. Frames are grouped by scene: a mono gold-ink "SCENE 01" label, an Anton scene name and the scene's time range on a hairline. Each frame is a 16:9 card with a dark code chip ("3C"), then the timecode, the action, and the dialogue or sound cue (speaker in a small mono label, one line per cue, quotes set as typographic quotes on screen only). Frames pair up two per row. A frame flagged `meta.feature`, and the last of any odd run, spans the row at full width as a hero shot, so no row ever has a hole. "Request a change" opens an inline note with an explicit Save. A saved note turns the card gold with a "Change requested" box (Edit note / Remove, and Remove asks twice). An unsaved note survives a reload.
  - **The bottom bar:** it stays hidden over the intro, then walks her through the scenes ("Scene 03 of 08 · Next scene"). The gold "Approve storyboard" / "Send N changes to Nelson" appears only once the last frame has been on screen. The confirm restates the story lock and the deposit. Focus starts on "Keep reviewing", and "Yes" ignores taps for 650ms, so a double tap can't approve. On phones the bar steps aside while a note is open; that's driven by the open/close taps, never by focus, because a style change mid-tap drops the tap on Save.
  - **After approving:** if Nelson has pasted a deposit link (the HoneyBook invoice) on the storyboard in admin, approving goes straight to it. The hero and the status rows then offer a gold "Pay your deposit". The copy says the story is set, so notes are about how a shot looks.
  - **Drafts:** a new storyboard is a draft (locked, never reviewed) that the client never sees until "Send to client" in admin. Older rounds fold into a read-only "Earlier version" row. Saving notes and sending the review happen in the database (`bk_storyboard_note` / `bk_storyboard_submit`, service role only via bk-media), so the lock and Nelson's message land together.
- **Held video (2026-10-29, first used for Delilah Amaro):** a delivered video waiting on its balance (`bk_galleries.unlock_invoice_id`) plays a watermarked preview with a gold "Preview" chip and no download; under it, one line of copy and the gold "Pay $X and download". Paying swaps in the clean file and Download on its own (never mid-play). Once the client has seen it, admin's "Stop client preview" (`preview_closed_at`) removes the video entirely: a still from the film, blurred in the file itself, under a radial ink scrim with one quiet label (lock + "Unlocks with your final payment"; a label, never a capsule that looks tappable), chip "Final cut", copy that sells the clean, full-quality file, and the hero's one gold button becomes the payment. Video names show as titles (no .mp4).
- **Files:** `css/client.css` (standalone), `js/client-portal.js`, and `js/gallery-core.js` with `mount(…, { v2: true })`. `portal-v1.html` is the previous portal for rollback (`portal.html?look=v1` redirects there); it and `gallery.html` keep the original gallery look. Test: `node tests/portal-v2.e2e.mjs` (stubbed; `PORTAL_IMAGES` / `PORTAL_SHOTS` / `PW_WEBKIT` optional).

## Inbox (inbox/), redesigned 2026-10-26 as a CRM in the Studio family
Product register. Nelson asked for his phone Inbox to "look more like a full functioning CRM" in his branding. It now shares the Studio dashboard's approved look (ink chrome, warm paper work surface, Anton / IBM Plex Sans Condensed / Familjen Grotesk / IBM Plex Mono; never Martian Mono).
- **Layout:** ink top bar (TAYLORMADE/CREATIVE + INBOX tag, gold Anton stats that double as filter shortcuts: Waiting on you, New leads this week, Unpaid invoices, Collected in <month>; Pitches, Studio ↗, Alerts, NT). Ink conversation list (search with "/", filters Needs reply / Leads / Booked / Delivered / Unpaid / All with counts, Today / Yesterday / This week / Earlier groups, monogram avatars with a gold dot when waiting, stage chips, "$ unpaid", gold left bar on the open row). Paper work surface with a rounded top-left corner: header (Project / ID, Anton name, service · company · "Asked for" or "Shoot" date, stage chip, Mark handled, Open in Studio), the thread (inquiry brief card first, day dividers, client bubbles light, his replies ink, mono event pills: invoice sent gold, paid green, problems red), the reply box docked under it, and a "Client & deal" column with numbered gold-circle cards (Client, Deal with an editable stage and Invoiced / Paid / Unpaid over a gold bar, Bookings, Earlier projects, Private notes, Copy portal link, Invoices & contracts in Studio).
- **Sizes:** ≥1180px three panes; 760–1179px list + work surface with Messages | Client & deal tabs (stats move into the list under 1440px); phones show one screen at a time with page scroll, a sticky slim bar (‹ Inbox · name once scrolled · Mark handled) and a sticky Send row (round Call and Text, Send fills the rest). A computer opens the top conversation by itself; a phone never does. A conversation opens on its newest message.
- **Send:** 5-second countdown that fills the gold button; the countdown belongs to the reply, so moving to another conversation keeps it going with a floating "Sending to <name> · Undo" bar (bottom-left on computers, under the top bar on phones). A reply that's only the greeting and "— Nelson" is refused. After sending, a phone returns to the list; a computer shows the reply in the thread with an empty box.
- **Data:** the same RPCs as before (bk_inbox_list / _item / _send / _mark) plus direct staff reads of bk_projects with invoices (as Studio does). Stage and private notes write bk_projects directly and ask for the row back so a refused write reads as a failure. No database changes.
- **Files:** `inbox/index.html`, `css/inbox-crm.css` (loads after `css/inbox.css`, which still styles Pitches), `js/inbox-crm.js`, `js/inbox-crm-view.js`. `inbox/v1.html` is the previous look (`/inbox/?look=v1` redirects there). Module imports are versioned (`?v=`) so a cached older module can't stop the app. Tests: `node tests/inbox-crm.e2e.mjs` (stubbed, desktop/laptop/tablet/phones; `SHOTS=<dir>` saves stills), `deno test tests/inbox-crm-view_test.js`, plus the existing inbox, drafts and pitches suites.

## Content calendar (content.html), added 2026-10-26
Product register, same family as the Inbox CRM (it loads `css/inbox-crm.css` for the chrome, then `css/content.css`). Nelson asked for his content calendar "built out in my CRM so I can easily find it", with the scripts going to Apple Notes and into Taylormade Camera's teleprompter.
- **Layout:** ink top bar (TAYLORMADE/CREATIVE + CONTENT tag, gold Anton stats that are the status filters: To film, To post, Posted n/total, the one that's on gets a gold wash; Inbox ↗, Studio ↗). Filter pills are All + the lanes (Apps, Photo, Direction). Ink day list grouped by Monday-to-Sunday week ("This week / Next week" + date range), one row per post: a date tile (gold when it's today), title, lane · format, status chip, gold left bar on the open row. Paper work surface: crumb, Anton title, lane · format · pillar, and a four-step status control (Planned, Filmed, Edited, Posted; posted fills green). Numbered gold-circle cards: 01 Hook & cover (with a Film by / Cut by day, the day before it posts), 02 Script, 03 Shot list on the left; 04 Captions, 05 Before you post, 06 Your notes on the right (one column below 1180px).
- **Script:** numbered beats (the shot list's "Beat 3" points at beat 3), word count and read time. The action row sits at the top of the card: where the script lives in Apple Notes (`notes_title` holds the whole path, folder › note) and a gold Copy script that copies spoken lines only, a blank line between beats, the way the camera app's script field wants them.
- **Captions:** Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn / Threads toggle (a platform without a caption is disabled), a suggested time for that platform and day, from his daily content engine playbook (LinkedIn 8:30 AM weekdays, 10 AM weekends; Instagram 11:30; Threads 11:45; TikTok 6 PM Central), Copy caption at the top, the character count against the platform's limit under the text.
- **Before you post:** a standing reminder for reels (music added in the app) above the post's own things to confirm. Plain dots, not boxes: nothing there is tickable.
- **Gold** stays on actions, today, the open row and the active stat. Beat numbers are faint mono, shot codes are ink-outline chips.
- **Phones:** list first; a row opens the post with a slim sticky bar (‹ Calendar, the title once it scrolls away); the browser Back button returns to the list. Copy buttons are full width and 48px; status steps are 44px.
- **Data:** `bk_content_posts` (admin-only RLS through `bk_is_admin()`, `bk_touch` keeps updated_at). Status and notes write directly and ask for the row back, so a refused write reads as a failure and the status snaps back. Notes save themselves 1.2s after typing stops and when the box loses focus (Save note and ⌘↩ still work); an unsaved note survives switching posts and reloads (sessionStorage), and a quiet reload when the tab comes back keeps what's being typed. Rapid status taps are sequenced: a stale answer is ignored, and a failed save re-reads the row instead of guessing. Re-rendered button groups keep keyboard focus. An expired sign-in sends him to sign in rather than blaming the connection. This repo is public: the posts themselves are written to the database through the API and never committed.
- **Files:** `content.html`, `css/content.css`, `js/content.js`, `js/content-view.js`, migration `20261026_bk_content_calendar.sql`. Tests: `deno test tests/content-view_test.js`, `node tests/content.e2e.mjs` (stubbed; `SHOTS=<dir>` saves stills), `node tests/content-calendar.pglite.mjs` (RLS and checks on real Postgres).
